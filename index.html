COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to
know.

Don’t see your question? Send an assessment request and we’ll point you in the right direction.

Can you guarantee recovery?+

No. Recoverability depends on the circumstances, the condition of the device or account, and any platform requirements. We give a clear assessment before proceeding.

Do you need my password or verification code?+

No. Never share a password, one-time passcode, recovery code, or payment-card details through the form or chat. We will explain any secure verification steps separately.

What proof of ownership is required?+

Requirements vary by case. We will only request appropriate verification through a secure, private process after the initial assessment.

Which devices and platforms do you support?+

Support depends on the case and current platform processes. Tell us your device or platform in the assessment form and we’ll confirm eligibility.
